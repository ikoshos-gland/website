<DropCap>C</DropCap>itations now work like Zotero. You write a key in the text, and the
reference list at the bottom builds itself from `content/references.bib`.

## How to cite

Flood-filling networks reconstruct neurons voxel by voxel
[@januszewskiHighprecisionAutomatedReconstruction2018]. A different school of
thought predicts local shape descriptors instead [@sheridanLocalShapeDescriptors2023],
and both pipelines start from expansion-microscopy volumes [@chenExpansionMicroscopy2015].

You can cite two works at once
[@januszewskiHighprecisionAutomatedReconstruction2018; @sheridanLocalShapeDescriptors2023],
or pin a page: [@chenExpansionMicroscopy2015, p. 544].
